DS3231 + AT24C32 RTC Breakout

High-Precision Real-Time Clock & 32Kb I²C EEPROM Module (Model ZS-042)

I²C Bus TCXO ±2ppm 3.3V – 5.5V

1. Module Overview & Features

The ZS-042 breakout board combines the Maxim/Analog Devices DS3231 high-precision Real-Time Clock with an Atmel/Microchip AT24C32 32-Kbit EEPROM memory chip. It features an integrated Temperature-Compensated Crystal Oscillator (TCXO) with an internal 32.768 kHz resonator, maintaining precision across temperatures.

  • Clock Accuracy: ±2 ppm (0°C to +40°C), ±3.5 ppm (-40°C to +85°C)
  • Operating Voltage: 2.3V to 5.5V DC (3.3V and 5.0V compatible)
  • Active Current: ∼200 μA; Timekeeping Standby: ≤1.5 μA
  • Memory: 32 Kbit (4,096 x 8-bit) non-volatile I²C EEPROM
  • Calendar Features: Seconds, minutes, hours, day, date, month, year with leap-year compensation valid up to year 2100
  • Alarms & Waveform: 2 programmable alarms, 32.768 kHz & configurable square-wave interrupt outputs

DS3231 Module Circuit & Battery Modification

CR2032 vs LIR2032 Safe Operation & Schematic Analysis

Hardware Safety Guide
CRITICAL BATTERY SAFETY NOTICE: DIODE MODIFICATION REQUIRED FOR CR2032 The ZS-042 breakout board comes factory-wired with an active trickle-charging circuit designed only for rechargeable lithium-ion LIR2032 cells (3.6V nominal, 4.2V max). If you install a standard, non-rechargeable CR2032 Lithium Manganese Dioxide coin cell (3.0V primary battery) and power the module with 5V, continuous charging current will be forced through diode D1 and resistor R1 (201) directly into the non-rechargeable cell. Over time, this causes electrolyte boiling, gas generation, battery swelling, chemical leakage, and potential cell rupture.

5. How to Modify Board for CR2032

  1. Locate diode D1 (labeled with red line/triangle, adjacent to resistor marked 201) near the positive battery terminal pad.
  2. Method A (Recommended): Touch a soldering iron tip to both ends of D1 and flick it off the PCB pads.
  3. Method B: Snip or crush diode D1 in half using micro flush cutters to break the circuit.
  4. Alternative: Alternatively desolder or remove resistor 201 (200 Ω).
