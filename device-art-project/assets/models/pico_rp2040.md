# Raspberry Pi Pico 3D CAD Model

- **Direct GitHub 3D Viewer Link:** [models/pico_rp2040.step](../../../models/pico_rp2040.step)
- **Source:** Official Raspberry Pi Foundation Design Portal (`Pico-R3.step`)
- **Format:** STEP AP214 (Solid Body, 1.6 MB)
- **Dimensions:** $51.0\text{ mm} \times 21.0\text{ mm} \times 3.0\text{ mm}$ (M2.5 mounting holes at $47.0\text{ mm} \times 11.4\text{ mm}$ pitch)
- **Features:** RP2040 dual-core ARM Cortex-M0+ MCU, 2MB SPI flash, micro-USB connector, 40 castellated DIP pins.
