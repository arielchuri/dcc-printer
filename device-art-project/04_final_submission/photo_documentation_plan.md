## 1. Required Photographs Matrix

| Shot # | Classification | Subject & Composition | Lighting & Camera Settings | Storytelling Intent |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Hero View** | 3/4 isometric perspective of the complete *DCC Printer* on a weathered dark oak gaming table, with a freshly printed character receipt curling out of the slot. | 50mm lens, f/4.0, ISO 100, 1/125s.<br>Key light 45° diffuse softbox, rim accent edge light highlighting acrylic finger joints. | Establishes the artifact’s sculptural presence, clean proportions, and evocative aesthetic. |
| **02** | **Detail View** | Tight macro close-up of the top operator console: knurled aluminum knob index line pointing to `[01]`, character LCD displaying green-yellow glyphs, and illuminated triangle button glowing softly. | 90mm macro lens, f/5.6, ISO 100.<br>Shallow depth-of-field emphasizing component textures (knurled metal, acrylic matte face). | Demonstrates high-quality tactile ergonomics and precision digital-fabrication fit. |
| **03** | **Craftsmanship / Joinery View** | Rear/side low-angle perspective highlighting the T-slot captive nut joints, visible M3 socket-head cap screws, acoustic ventilation grilles, and DC barrel jack. | 35mm lens, f/8.0 (deep focus), ISO 100.<br>Strong rim light catching the laser-cut edge bevels and star-ground stud. | Celebrates honest mechanical joinery, serviceability, and industrial fabrication quality. |
| **04** | **In-Use / Human Interaction View** | Human hands actively engaging the machine: left hand steadying the chassis while right thumb depresses the illuminated triangle button; paper beginning to emerge. | 50mm lens, f/2.8, ISO 200, 1/250s.<br>Warm atmospheric lighting capturing human contact and kinetic anticipation. | Illustrates the live interactive encounter and physical scale relative to human hands. |

## 2. Post-Processing & Exhibition Print Specifications

- **Color Profile:** Display P3 / sRGB (Neutral white balance balanced for 5000K daylight).
- **Resolution:** Minimum $4000 \times 3000\text{ px}$ (300 DPI print-ready for exhibition catalogue / Learning Portfolio gallery).
- **Archival Export:** Master uncompressed 16-bit TIFF + optimized web JPGs.
