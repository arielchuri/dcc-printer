**Audio:** Live ambient mechanical sound + Voiceover narration

## 2. Technical Production Notes

- **Lighting Setup:** 3-point lighting with key softbox at 45° camera-left, rim hair-light highlighting the matte acrylic bevel edges, and diffused fill.
- **Microphone Placement:** Røde directional shotgun mic positioned 18 inches above the printer slot to capture the acoustic texture of the stepper motor, microswitch snap, and paper tear.
