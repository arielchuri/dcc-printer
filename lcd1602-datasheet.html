5. Hardware Controls & Adjustment Guide

Contrast Potentiometer (10 kΩ): If the display shows black boxes or is blank on first power-up, use a small flathead/Phillips screwdriver to rotate the blue potentiometer clockwise/counter-clockwise until character contrast is sharp.
Backlight Jumper Cap: Shunting the 2-pin jumper powers the LED backlight via the onboard transistor. Remove the jumper cap to fully disconnect backlight power or insert an external PWM signal for variable dimming.
